Short Bio: **SCOTT BAIER** is a Professor at Clemson University.  He is currently serving as the Associate Dean of Research in the Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business.   He holds a doctorate in economics from Michigan State University.  Prior to joining the faculty at Clemson, Professor Baier was an Assistant Professor at the University of Notre Dame. From 2007-2008, he served as a Senior Economist on the President’s Council of Economic Advisors where he advised President George W. Bush on matters related to the economy. From 1999-2012, Scott was a visiting scholar at the Federal Reserve Bank in Atlanta. More recently, he has also served as a consultant to the Congressional Budget Office (CBO) and the United States International Trade Commission (USITC). sampl.
